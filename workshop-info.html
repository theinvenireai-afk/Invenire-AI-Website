📚 Workshop Info

How Our Workshops Work

Two formats, one mission: making AI education accessible, exciting, and genuinely hands-on for every student we meet.

Workshop Formats

Choose Your Format

We offer two workshop formats — a multi-day deep-dive series and a single-day bootcamp. Both are free for nonprofit and publicly sponsored institutions. Flip a card to see the full schedule.

📅

Multi-Day Workshop

45 min – 1 hr · 4–5 Days

Our full-depth AI learning series, built day by day — from how computers see and learn, to creative collaboration, and finally a hands-on project students present to parents on the last day.

Each day explores a new theme — Algorithms, Machine Learning, Creative Collaboration, and two Project Days — using hands-on tools like Teachable Machine, Scratch, and AI Duet, plus icebreaker games that make big AI concepts click. Volunteers guide every station so every student gets real support.

📅 Full 5-Day Schedule

  • Day 1 — Basics of Algorithms: Pictionary AI, a quick intro to Scratch, and the "Intelligent Paper" algorithm activity.
  • Day 2 — Basics of Machine Learning: Semantris for language patterns, plus hands-on training with Code.org and Google Teachable Machine.
  • Day 3 — Creative Collaboration: AI Duet music-making and building a personalized chatbot with Landbot.
  • Day 4 — Project Day: The "Human Neural Network" game, then small-group projects — Machine Learning for Kids, Face Sensing, and Smart Classroom in Scratch.
  • Day 5 — Project Day: Final work time, a showcase for parents, and take-home resources to keep learning.
⚡

Single-Day Bootcamp

3–4 Hours · One Day

The complete AI experience, condensed into one energizing session. In just a few hours, students go from training their first computer-vision model to building a working mini AI project — no prior experience needed, just curiosity.

The day blends hands-on tech, unplugged games, and a final project students get to show off, so even first-timers leave having actually built something with AI.

⚡ Full Session Breakdown

  • Phase 1 — How Computers See: The Quick, Draw! doodle game, then training a Teachable Machine model to tell happy vs. silly faces apart.
  • Phase 2 — How Computers Talk & Create: An unplugged "Human Neural Network" game, plus AI Duet and Semantris for music and language.
  • Phase 3 — Mini-Project: Building a "Smart Classroom" in Scratch with Machine Learning for Kids, followed by a gallery walk to see everyone's projects.
  • Phase 4 — Wrap-Up: A Junior AI Scientist certificate, plus a take-home QR code so families can keep exploring.
